Our services

Nine specialized treatments,
one personal approach

Every treatment is available individually or combined into a plan built specifically for your recovery.

01

Heat & Cryotherapy

Controlled hot and cold treatment to ease pain, reduce swelling and relax tight muscles before deeper work begins.

02

Ultrasound Therapy

Therapeutic sound waves that reach deep tissue to reduce inflammation and speed up natural healing.

03

Electrical Stimulation (TENS & IFT)

Neuromuscular electrical stimulation used to relieve pain and re-activate weakened muscles.

04

Immobilization

Plaster application and splinting to protect and stabilize an injury while it heals.

05

Exercise Therapy

Structured, progressive movement programs that rebuild strength, balance and flexibility.

06

Therapeutic Massage

Manual soft-tissue techniques to relieve tension, improve circulation and support recovery.

07

Proprioceptive Neuromuscular Facilitation

PNF stretching techniques that improve flexibility and restore coordinated muscle function.

08

Neuro-Developmental Therapy

Specialized rehabilitation for neurological conditions, focused on retraining movement patterns.

09

Orthotics & Mobility Aids

Lumbar, knee and wrist supports, plus crutches, wheelchairs, walking frames and walking sticks.
