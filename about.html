Meet Susan

A hands-on approach, built on genuine attention

Susan Akinyi founded Suel Physiotherapy to offer Kisumu residents accessible, personal rehabilitation care — the kind that treats you as a person, not a case number.

Every client is assessed, treated and followed up by Susan herself, from the first consultation through full recovery. That continuity means your treatment plan evolves with you, session by session, instead of being handed between different therapists.

Her practice covers everything from post-surgical rehabilitation and chronic pain management to sports injuries and neurological recovery — using evidence-based, drug-free techniques tailored to each person's body and goals.
